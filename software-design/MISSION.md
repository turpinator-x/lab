# Mission: Strategic software design, for working with AI agents

## Why
Show employers the quality and care already visible in my projects, and speak to it in an interview. Coding syntax is the "tactical" part agents now handle; deciding how a system is shaped is the "strategic" part that stays mine.

## Success looks like
- Explain in plain words what a deep module is, why shallow modules and complexity hurt, and give an example from my own projects.
- Explain ubiquitous language, bounded contexts and a glossary, and show how my course repo uses them.
- Explain how to change code safely without understanding all of it (characterization tests, seams), tied to how I review agent work.
- Answer "how do you keep AI-written code good?" with a clear, short story in my own voice.

## Constraints
- I won't buy or read the three books in full: short plain lessons, diagrams, audio-friendly, no walls of text.
- Weekly Claude usage is nearly spent until the Sunday 08:00 reset: keep sessions small.
- Summaries are in our own words from free primary sources; no copying from the books.

## Out of scope
- Learning a programming language (Python and others), line-by-line code.
- Buying or reading the full books.
