# Glossary

The agreed words for the BlockOne DevOps AI Mentorship Journey. One word per concept. If you need a word that isn't here, add it here first.

## Rule: never write "progress" alone

Write **learning progress** or **career advancement**. Bare "progress" is allowed only in a file or field name, such as `progress.json`.

## Terms

**Learner**:
The person using the curriculum. Every learner is a lifelong learner, and learners are expected to become mentors in the field and pay it forward.
_Avoid_: student, trainee

**Mentor**:
The AI that teaches and guides a learner toward becoming a DevOps engineer, and cares about the whole learner. A role the AI plays, not a person. "Teacher" is an allowed alias.
_Avoid_: tutor, bot

**Maintainer**:
The role of a person or AI agent that changes the curriculum, the software or the mentor's teaching style.
_Avoid_: developer, editor

**Curriculum**:
Everything that is taught: stages, lessons, tracks, electives and certification tracks.
_Avoid_: course, program

**Journey**:
The learner's whole experience: learning the curriculum, certifications, interviewing, getting a job, and building a career.
_Avoid_: path

**Stage**:
A group of lessons that builds one set of skills. Core stages teach the day-to-day skills needed to be useful on day one of a DevOps job.
_Avoid_: module, course

**Lesson**:
One unit of teaching, 50 minutes or less.
_Avoid_: class, chapter

**Elective**:
An optional stage outside the core. A career enhancer.
_Avoid_: bonus, extra

**Guide**:
A document that gives the learner direction, such as a map of where the curriculum is going.
_Avoid_: manual

**Learning progress**:
How far a learner has come toward being interview and job ready, through the curriculum. In scope for the product.
_Avoid_: progress (alone)

**Career advancement**:
Growth in a career after getting a job. Outside the product's scope. The curriculum gives hints about it, such as job titles, because the field changes fast.
_Avoid_: progress (alone)
