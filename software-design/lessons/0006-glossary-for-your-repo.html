Write the glossary for your course repo

Why now

In Lesson 3 you picked one word over another. Now you do it for real, in blockone-devops-ai-journey. The result is a real artifact you can show an employer.

Your repo already has the rule: CLAUDE.md says to use "one word for one thing", and docs/language-plan.md says it again. What's missing is the list of which words. (I read CLAUDE.md, README.md, docs/language-plan.md and part of mentor/MENTOR.md.)

A real catch from your own words

In Lesson 5 you said "student". Your repo says learner. That is exactly the kind of drift a glossary prevents. An agent reading your repo uses "learner". You, speaking, use "student". Pick one.

Step 2: find a bounded context

Recall from Lesson 3: a bounded context starts where the language changes. Look at these top-level folders of your repo: curriculum/, mentor/, learner/, tests/, feedback/.

Ask for each: does a word like "progress" or "lesson" mean something different here? My guess, to check, not a fact: "lesson" is teaching material in curriculum/ but a test target in tests/. If it does, you have found a context boundary and you can name the mapping between them. If nothing changes, it's one context.
